# gravyard — Authorized Use Only

Everything in this repository is built for **learning and for use against
targets you own or have written permission to test**.

## The rule

> Do not run any gravyard tool against systems, networks, accounts, or data
> that you do not own or lack explicit written authorization to test.

## What that means per category

- **crypto** — safe on your own files, CTF challenges, and lab apps you run.
- **crawler / webtest / network** — only on your own sites, home lab, or
  in-scope engagement targets.
- **research / redteam-lab / reverse** — isolated lab VMs and intentionally
  vulnerable targets only (OverTheWire, HackTheBox/TryHackMe, DVWA, Juice
  Shop, crackmes.one, CTF binaries). No internet-facing hosts.

## Legal reality

Unauthorized access, scanning, interception, and password cracking are
crimes in most jurisdictions — in India, the Information Technology Act,
2000 (§43, §66) and the Penal Code; elsewhere, equivalents such as the US
CFAA. "It was just a lab tool" is not a defense; authorization and scope
are.

## Standard practice

1. Get written authorization before testing anything you don't own.
2. Stay inside the agreed scope (targets, methods, time windows).
3. Keep proof of authorization with your engagement notes.
4. Report findings to the owner; never use them beyond the agreed purpose.
